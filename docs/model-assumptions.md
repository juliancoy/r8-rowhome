# Model Assumptions

- The default rowhome is a three-story, all-electric Baltimore R-8 concept model.
- The model uses feet as its exported geometry unit.
- Gas piping and gas appliances are intentionally omitted.
- Electrical devices are schematic geometry with source-traced metadata.
- Site drainage and erosion-control concepts are metadata-level annotations, not civil engineering plans.
- Historic district requirements are not applied unless a future preset identifies a CHAP-governed site.
- Outputs are visualization and fabrication-study artifacts, not construction documents.
